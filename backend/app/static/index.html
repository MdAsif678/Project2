            📖 Aegis-Q Cryptographic Glossary & Guide
          

          

            Plain-English reference for SIH26164 judges and security analysts
          

        

        
      


      

        

          

            1. What is TLS (Transport Layer Security)?
          

          

            TLS is the cryptographic protocol that powers HTTPS (the padlock icon in your browser). It encrypts all network traffic between your device and remote web servers.
          

        


        

          

            2. What is a CBOM (Cryptography Bill of Materials)?
          

          

            A standardized machine-readable inventory (OWASP CycloneDX v1.6) listing every encryption algorithm, certificate, and post-quantum risk level in an application.
          

        


        

          

            3. What is Shor's Algorithm?
          

          

            A quantum algorithm that solves discrete logarithms and prime factorization in polynomial time $O(n^3)$. This means RSA, ECDSA, and ECDH are completely broken on quantum computers.
          

        


        

          

            4. What is Grover's Algorithm?
          

          

            A quantum search algorithm that halves symmetric encryption key lengths: AES-128 drops to 64-bit security (vulnerable), whereas AES-256 drops to ~128-bit security (still safe!).
          

        


        

          

            5. What are NIST FIPS 203 & FIPS 204?
          

          

            Official Post-Quantum standards finalized by NIST in 2024:
            
• FIPS 203 (ML-KEM / Kyber): Lattice-based Key Encapsulation (replaces ECDH / RSA key exchange).
            
• FIPS 204 (ML-DSA / Dilithium): Lattice-based Signatures (replaces ECDSA / RSA signatures).
          

        


        

          

            6. What is HNDL (Harvest Now, Decrypt Later)?
          

          

            Adversaries collecting and storing encrypted communications today to retroactively decrypt them when quantum hardware is online.
